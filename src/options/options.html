Engine

Lara meters usage in characters, two different ways. The full-image engine bills a FLAT 10,000 characters per image, so 40 pages is about 400,000 characters — enough to exhaust a Pro month (500,000) and far beyond the 10,000 chars/month of API access on the Free plan. The “Lens OCR + Lara text” engine bills only the characters actually sent — a manga page is usually 500–1,500 — so the same allowance covers roughly 10–20 pages. Cached pages are always free. All four image modes bill the same flat 10,000 per image, so the model is a quality/speed choice, not a cost one: only inpainting removes the original text, which is what makes a comic read cleanly. Requests are sent with X-No-Trace, so Lara neither stores nor trains on your images. Get credentials.

Limits

A low delay gets rate-limited by Google quickly. 1500 ms is a reasonable floor.

Privacy

Any image processed is uploaded to Google for OCR and translation — that is inherent to using this extension. Requests are anonymous: no cookies or account data are sent.
